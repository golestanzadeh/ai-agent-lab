# D-025 Tax Calculation Progress

Date: 2026-09-12
Status: D025_ACTIVE

## Fail-closed calculation rules
- A deduction candidate is never represented as a refund amount without a tax-liability calculation.
- Conditional candidates remain conditional until statutory and evidence prerequisites are satisfied.
- Direct tax credits under §35a are separated from deductions from taxable income.
- Union contributions under §9(1) Nr.3 / §9a sentence 3 are tracked separately from the Arbeitnehmer-Pauschbetrag.
- Cross-year costs are not forced into 2024.
- Reimbursements are reconciled before travel or medical costs become claim-ready.

## Current bounded components
- Evidenced union payments: EUR 246.07, incremental to employee lump sum; earlier months remain an upward evidence gap.
- Gas-maintenance §35a base: EUR 90.00; conditional direct credit EUR 18.00 after qualifying account-payment proof.
- Explicit operating-cost §35a candidate base: EUR 69.13; provisional maximum 20% component EUR 13.83 pending row classification/evidence.
- Conditional school-fee deduction: EUR 144.00 if EUR 480 qualifying tuition was actually paid in 2024 and §10(1) Nr.9 conditions are met.
- Dental gross candidate: EUR 608.17; effective §33 amount unresolved pending reimbursements and reasonable-burden calculation.
- Donation and WISO remain unresolved and are not booked into 2024.

## High-value unresolved calculation
Bus-driver travel/per-diem remains the principal opportunity gap. It requires duty-day history, employer assignment/Sammelpunkt facts and reconciliation to the EUR 1,002.21 tax-free employer reimbursement before a defensible amount can be calculated.

No ELSTER or Finanzamt transmission is part of D-025.
