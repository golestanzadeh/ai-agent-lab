# Tax Analysis and Calculation Policy

Status: **CANONICAL**

This document preserves the durable analytical rules established during D-024 and D-025. Historical progress reports from those stages are not current project state.

## Source discipline

- NO SOURCE -> NO TAX CLAIM.
- NO SOURCE -> NO OPPORTUNITY.
- Model memory, blogs, tax-software convention, and unsupported inference are not authority.
- Use the law and official guidance applicable to the exact tax year.
- Preferred authority order: applicable statutes/regulations; official BMF material; official ELSTER form help; applicable BFH/BVerfG case law.
- Separate fact, evidence, legal authority, inference, uncertainty, calculation, and ELSTER placement.
- Missing evidence is never silently converted to zero or to an invented amount.
- Consequential ambiguity or irreconcilable conflict fails closed to HUMAN_REQUIRED.

## Calculation discipline

- Separate confirmed amounts, conditional candidates, deductions from taxable income, and direct tax credits.
- Reconcile employer and third-party reimbursements before claiming an expense.
- Keep cross-year items unresolved until payment, service, and reference-year treatment is evidenced.
- Distinguish payroll baseline, Werbungskosten and lump-sum interaction, travel treatment, section 35a credits, Sonderausgaben, extraordinary burdens, Vorsorgeaufwendungen, advisory/software costs, and opportunity scenarios.
- Produce an estimated refund only when filing status and all material inputs permit it.
- Preserve case, run, evidence, source, calculation, review, and form-placement provenance.

## Authority boundary

Analysis and calculation do not authorize declaration signing, ELSTER transmission, Finanzamt contact, evidence mutation, protected-main merge, or any irreversible tax action. Those actions remain behind their explicit Human Gates.
